Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This policy explains what LiveIT does with your personal data, why, and what you can do about it. It covers the LiveIT app from version 1.0.0 and the LiveIT web pages.

In short

  • We use what the game needs: your account, your proofs and how you play.
  • Other players see your profile and your proofs. Only what you share yourself leaves the app.
  • No ads, no tracking, no selling of data.
  • LiveIT is for people 16 and over. We never store your year of birth.
  • You can delete your account in the app at any time. Your photos and videos go with it.

1. Who is responsible

The controller of your personal data is Martin Stříž, who runs LiveIT. In this policy “we” means him.

Email: support@joinliveit.com

Write to this address about anything in this policy. We answer in English, Slovak and Czech.

2. What we collect

Your account

  • Your email address and password. Supabase keeps the password only in a scrambled form that no one can read back.
  • Your username and, if you add one, a profile photo. If you sign up by email, the username is the one you type. If you sign in with Apple or Google, it starts as a random one such as player_482913, never anything from your email address, until you choose your own in Settings.
  • Your phone’s time zone (for example Europe/Prague), so your day starts at your midnight.
  • If you sign in with Apple: the email address Apple gives us. It can be a private relay address. We never store a name from Apple.
  • If you sign in with Google: your email address, your name and the web address of your Google profile photo. We keep them with your account and never show them to anyone else, and the Google photo never becomes your profile photo. If your account was made before this version of the policy, your username may come from your email address and your profile photo may be the Google one. You can change both in Settings.
  • That you confirmed you are 16 or over, and when. Not your year of birth.

What you post

  • Proofs: a photo from the camera or your photo library, with a small thumbnail for your collection, or a video of up to 15 seconds filmed in the app, with its sound, and the caption you write.
  • Comments, Respect, who you follow and who you block.
  • Reports you send, with the reason and any note you add.
  • With each proof the app also sends whether it came from the camera or the gallery, when it was taken (from the photo’s own data) and a fingerprint of the file. This keeps the same photo from counting again and again.
  • Every photo, a proof or a profile photo, is re-encoded on your phone before upload, which drops hidden details such as the place it was taken. If that does not work, nothing is uploaded. Videos can only be filmed in the app, and the app saves no location in them. Test versions from before the public release did not always do this. They uploaded a video from your gallery, and now and then a photo, as it was, so such a file can still hold the place where it was taken. Delete that proof in the app and its file goes with it, or write to us and we delete it.

How you play

  • The cards you get and the quest you pick, XP, level, streak, umbrellas, badges, your collection, quick wins, weekly events and leagues.
  • The days you play, and when each proof and quick win happened.
  • Notifications inside the app, for example when someone gives you Respect.

Technical data

  • Supabase, which runs our servers, keeps short logs of requests and sign-ins, such as IP address, time and app version, for security. It deletes them after a few days.
  • When the app checks for an update, it sends Expo a random install ID made on your phone, with the platform and the app version. This ID is not linked to your account.
  • Apple may share crash reports and anonymous app statistics with us, but only if you turned on Share With App Developers in your iPhone’s settings.

Error reports from the app

  • When something goes wrong in the app, it sends us a short report: the kind of error, the error text and the place in the app’s code where it happened, the screen it happened on (such as the feed or a profile, never whose), the app version, the platform (iOS or Android) and the version of your phone’s operating system.
  • Before sending, the app takes email addresses, IDs, the details in web addresses and long numbers out of the text, and our server does it again. What we store holds no account, no username and no IP address. Reports of the same error are counted together.
  • So that we see it at once, an anonymous alert with the kind of error, the screen, the app version and the error text also goes to our private Discord channel (section 5).

When you write to us

  • Your email address and your message.

Feedback you send in the app

  • When you report a bug or send an idea in the app: whether it is a bug or an idea, your text, the app version, the platform (iOS or Android) and the language of the app. All of it is linked to your account.
  • So that we see it at once, a notice with your username and your text also goes to our private Discord channel (section 5).

Our web pages

  • When you open the LiveIT website or these pages, the host (Cloudflare) handles your IP address and browser details to deliver the page and keep it safe.
  • An invite page reads the @username from its own address, only in your browser. We set no cookies.

What we do not collect

  • No contacts. You add friends by their exact username.
  • No GPS or other precise location. The app never asks for it. Only a file from a test version, as described above, can still hold a place.
  • No date of birth and no year of birth.
  • No advertising IDs and no tracking across other apps or websites. The app has no analytics or advertising tools.
  • Your language and your daily reminder stay on your phone. The phone schedules the reminder itself, and only if you turn it on.
  • The app uses the camera, the microphone and the photo library only when you take or pick a proof or change your profile photo.

3. Why, and on what legal basis

To run LiveIT for you

Your account, the daily cards, proofs, XP, streaks, badges, your collection, friends, leagues, and the emails that confirm your address or reset your password. Legal basis: the contract with you, which is our Terms (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

To show your profile and proofs to other players

This is how LiveIT works. Legal basis: the contract.

To check that you are 16 or over

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in keeping LiveIT to people 16 and over, as our Terms require (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

To keep LiveIT safe and fair

Reports, blocking, the automatic hiding after three reports, the filter for prohibited words in usernames, captions and comments, limits on how often an account can act, the checks on each proof, and records of what we decided. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in a safe community and in handling reports as the law and the App Store require.

To learn whether LiveIT works

In our own database we count sign-ups, how many people come back, add friends and post proofs, which country they likely play in by time zone, and through whose invite new people came. When we start LiveIT with a group of friends, we note their usernames and the date. Some of these counts look at single accounts. We use them only to understand and improve LiveIT, never for ads, and we share them with no one. Every morning, and on Mondays for the past week, the totals for the whole app go to our private Discord channel, with no names. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in improving LiveIT. You can object (section 13).

To find and fix errors in the app

The error reports show us which errors happen, on which screen and in which version, so that we can fix them. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in an app that works.

To deliver app updates

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in fixing the app quickly.

To answer your messages

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in helping you, or the contract when it is about your account.

To handle the feedback you send in the app

We read every bug and idea you send, fix what is broken, use the ideas to make LiveIT better and, if we need to ask you something, write to you by email. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in helping you and improving LiveIT.

To keep records and defend legal claims

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in being able to show how we handled reports and deletions.

To post your proof outside the app

Only with your consent for that one post (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). See section 11.

You need an email address, or Apple or Google sign-in, to create an account, and you need to answer the age step to use the app. Without them LiveIT cannot work. Everything else is up to you.

4. Who sees what in the app

  • Every signed-in player can see your username, profile photo, level and title, streak, badges, collection, your proofs with their captions, your comments, the Respect your proofs got, your XP on the leaderboards, and who you follow and who follows you.
  • People who follow you see your proofs in their Friends tab. All players see them in the Everyone tab.
  • If you block someone, or someone blocks you, you no longer see each other.
  • Your email address is never shown to other players.
  • Bugs and ideas you send in the app are seen only by us.
  • Photo and video files are stored at web addresses that are hard to guess. Anyone who has the exact address of a file can open it. The app shows these addresses only to signed-in players.
  • Nothing leaves the app unless you share it yourself, for example as a story card or a sticker.

Discord (Discord Netherlands BV, and Discord Inc., USA)

Delivers to our private channels the alert about each new report (section 9), a daily and a weekly summary of totals for the whole app, alerts when something in LiveIT breaks, and the feedback you send in the app. A report alert holds only the kind of item, the reason, the first characters of the report’s random ID, how many reports are open and whether the item was hidden. The summaries name no one either, and neither do the alerts about errors in the app, which hold the kind of error, the screen, the app version, the platform and the error text without personal details. Cloudflare, Inc., USA

Hosts the LiveIT website and these legal pages, and forwards the email you send to support@joinliveit.com to our mailbox. Gets your IP address and browser details when you open a page. If we count visits, we use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which sets no cookies.

Apple (Apple Distribution International Ltd., Ireland, and Apple Inc., USA)

Runs the App Store and Sign in with Apple. Apple is responsible for this itself, under its own privacy policy.

Google (Google Ireland Ltd., and Google LLC, USA)

Authorities

When personal data goes to the USA, the transfer rests on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where the company is certified under it, or on the standard contractual clauses of the European Commission in its data processing terms. Write to us for a copy of these safeguards.

7. How long we keep data

  • Your account and everything in it: until you delete your account.
  • A proof you delete: its photo or video is deleted at once and the post disappears for everyone. A hidden record of the quest (which quest, when, and the caption) stays with your account until you delete the account, so the same quest is not offered again and points stay fair.
  • Reports, and the records of what we decided about them: three years after the decision. These records hold random IDs of accounts and posts, the reason, the note added to the report, if any, and the time, but no emails or photos.
  • The record that an account was deleted: three years. It holds only the random ID of the account and the time.
  • Technical counters that limit how often an account can act: at most three years. They hold an account ID, an action and a time.
  • An account we stop for breaking the Terms: it can no longer sign in, and its proofs and comments are hidden. Other players still see its username and profile photo. If these break the rules too, we delete the photo and give the account a neutral username. We keep the account while we need it for that case, at most three years, then delete it.
  • Server logs at Supabase: a few days.
  • Backups of the database: we make them before every change to the database and once a week. They hold no photos or videos, stay on our own encrypted computer, and we delete each one after 30 days. Data you deleted can stay in a backup until then.
  • Your consent to post a proof outside the app: while the post is up, and three years after, so we can show we had it.
  • Emails you send us: as long as we need them to help you.
  • Feedback you send in the app: as long as we need it to deal with it, at most 12 months, and at once when you delete your account. Its notice in our Discord channel we delete when we have dealt with it, at the latest 12 months after you sent it.
  • Error reports from the app: at most 14 days after the error last happened. Their alerts in our Discord channel we delete when we have dealt with them, at the latest 12 months after they were sent.
  • Data on your phone: until you delete the app.

Three years is the usual time limit for legal claims in Czech and Slovak law.

8. Deleting your account

In the app: You → Settings (the sliders icon at the top right) → Safety → Delete account → Delete forever → Delete my account.

This deletes at once your sign-in and email address, your profile and username, every photo and video you uploaded (with thumbnails and profile photos), your proofs and captions, comments, Respect, follows and blocks, notifications, badges, collection, streak, game history, the reports you sent and the feedback you sent in the app.

What stays: the record that an account was deleted, the records and counters from section 7 (random IDs only), the reports other people sent about your proofs or your profile (with their reason and note, but no longer linked to you), the notices of your feedback in our Discord channel until we delete them (section 7), error reports, which hold no account and no IP address, and copies in backups until they are deleted.

Without the app: write to support@joinliveit.com from the address of your account. We delete it within one month at the latest.

9. Reports and moderation

  • Every proof, comment and profile has a report option with seven reasons. You can also block any account.
  • A report reaches us at once, as an alert in our private Discord channel. The alert names no one and contains no photo or text.
  • When three different accounts report the same proof or comment, it is hidden automatically until we look at it. Profiles are never hidden automatically.
  • We act on every report within 24 hours. We hide content that breaks our Terms, delete a profile photo that breaks them and replace such a username with a neutral one. We stop the accounts of people who break the Terms. Content hidden by mistake can come back.
  • To decide, we look at the reported content, the reason and the note. Hidden content stays stored, photo included, as evidence until its author deletes it or the account is deleted. A photo the law does not let us keep online, such as a sexual photo of a child or an intimate photo shared without consent, we delete sooner. If the police ask us to keep it, we keep it only for them, out of public reach.
  • If needed, we write to the person who reported or to the author by email. We never tell anyone who reported them.

10. Age

LiveIT is for people 16 and over. After you sign in, the app asks once for your year of birth.

  • If the year means you are 16 or over, the app sends it to our server once to check it. The server keeps only that you confirmed it, and when. The year is never stored.
  • If the year means you are under 16, the year never leaves your phone. The app asks once whether the year is right, and then deletes the account.

If you think someone under 16 uses LiveIT, write to us and we will delete the account.

11. Your proofs outside the app

Your proofs stay in the app unless you share them yourself. We post a proof on LiveIT’s own social media, such as TikTok or Instagram, or send it to anyone outside the app, only with your explicit consent for that one post. We also need the consent of every person who can be recognised in it, or we blur them. We never show the faces of minors.

We keep a record of each consent. You can withdraw it at any time, and we then take the post down within 24 hours, 48 at the latest.

12. Automated decisions

We make no decisions about you that rest only on automated processing and have legal or similarly significant effects on you. Two things happen automatically: a proof or comment hides after three reports from different accounts, until we check it, and usernames, captions and comments are checked against a list of prohibited words before they are saved.

13. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • get a copy of your data and learn how we use it (Art. 15 GDPR),
  • have wrong data corrected (Art. 16). Most of it you can change in the app yourself,
  • have your data deleted (Art. 17),
  • have our use of your data restricted (Art. 18),
  • get the data you gave us in a machine-readable format (Art. 20),
  • object to any use that rests on our legitimate interest, including the counting in section 3 (Art. 21),
  • withdraw a consent at any time. This does not affect what happened before.

Write to support@joinliveit.com, ideally from the address of your account. We answer within one month, and it costs nothing. We may ask you to confirm that the account is yours.

You can also complain to a data protection authority:

  • Czech Republic: Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů, uoou.gov.cz
  • Slovakia: Úrad na ochranu osobných údajov Slovenskej republiky, dataprotection.gov.sk
  • or the authority in the EU country where you live or work.

14. Security

Data travels encrypted. The app keeps your sign-in in the phone’s secure storage. Rules on our server decide what each account can read and change, and a report can be seen only by the LiveIT admin and by the person who sent it. No method is perfectly safe, so use a strong password that you use nowhere else. If a breach puts your data at risk, we tell you and the authority as the law requires.

15. Changes

When we change this policy, we change the date at the top. If a change matters for you, we tell you in advance, in the app or by email.
