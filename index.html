Support

Every day you’re dealt three quest cards. Pick one, go and do it, and prove it with a photo. Then see what your friends did today.

Write to us

Questions, ideas, bugs, or anything about your account or your data:

support@joinliveit.com

We usually reply within a few days, in English, Slovak or Czech.

Your account and data

In the app, open You → Settings (the sliders icon at the top right). There you change your photo, username, language and password, see the accounts you blocked, and delete your account.

Delete your account

In the app: You → Settings → Safety → Delete account → Delete forever. This deletes your account, your proofs and every photo and video you uploaded, at once.

Without the app: write to support@joinliveit.com from the address of your account and ask us to delete it. We do it within one month at the latest and tell you when it is done. If you signed in with Apple and hid your email address, tell us your username.

What stays after deletion is listed in the Privacy Policy.

Report a problem

Every proof, comment and profile has a … menu with Report this post and Block user. We look at every report within 24 hours. If someone is in danger, call 112.

Age

LiveIT is for people 16 and over. After you sign in, the app asks once for your year of birth and keeps only the confirmation.

Contact point for users and authorities under the EU Digital Services Act: support@joinliveit.com, in English, Slovak or Czech.
